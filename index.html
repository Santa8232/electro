How to Use ElectroSim Pro

1. Adding Components: Drag any component directly from the left palette onto the canvas, or click the icon to add to center.

2. Wiring Terminals: Click any terminal dot to start a wire. The live rubber-band wire preview snaps to target terminals with visual feedback. Click the target terminal to complete connection.

3. Real Physics Current Flow: An integrated Modified Nodal Analysis (MNA) circuit engine calculates real node voltages and branch currents. Glowing electron particles travel in the actual physical direction of current. If a circuit is open, current stops!

4. Interactive Switches: Click any switch directly on the canvas to open or close it instantly. Watch the circuit react in real-time.

5. Rotating & Deleting: Select a component and press R or click the rotate icon to turn 90°. Press Delete or Backspace to delete.

6. Oscilloscope & Live Probing: Click any component or wire to probe its real-time voltage and current waveforms on the oscilloscope.

7. Undo, Save & Export: Use Ctrl+Z to undo and Ctrl+Y to redo. Save circuits locally or export/import JSON files from the top bar.
